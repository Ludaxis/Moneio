## The Problem: A $15B+ Market Pain

### The Cash Flow Crisis

According to the [U.S. Bank and NFIB](https://www.uschamber.com/co/start/strategy/why-small-businesses-fail), **82% of small business failures are due to cash flow mismanagement**. This isn't about bad business ideas—profitable companies die when they can't pay bills on time.

Additional data from [Bureau of Labor Statistics 2024](https://www.commerceinstitute.com/business-failure-rate/):
- **21.5% of businesses fail in year one**
- **49.4% fail within 5 years**
- **65.3% fail within 10 years**

### The Hidden Time Tax

Small business owners are losing their most valuable resource—time:

| Metric | Data | Source |
|--------|------|--------|

### The Financial Expertise Gap

According to a [Federal Reserve 2025 survey](https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/analysis/2025/2025-survey-of-business-resource-organizations):
- **Only 42% of small businesses have financing needs fully met**
- **53% of owners can't afford debt** at current interest rates (12.5-15.5% SBA loan rates)
- **57% say regulation is holding them back** — largely due to financial compliance complexity

The alternative? Pay for expertise:

*Sources: [NerdWallet 2025](https://www.nerdwallet.com/business/software/learn/bookkeeping-pricing), [Graphite Financial](https://graphitefinancial.com/blog/fractional-cfo-hourly-rates/)*
